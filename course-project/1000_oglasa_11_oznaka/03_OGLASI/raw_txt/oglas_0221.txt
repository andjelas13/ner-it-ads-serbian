Point of View GeForce GTX 680 (part number VGA-680-A1) desktop Graphics Processing Unit was released in March 2012. The card is based on NVIDIA GeForce GTX 680, and manufactured using 28 nm technological process. The graphics card is clocked at 1.006 GHz, and supports boost technology, that can increase operating frequency up to 1.058 GHz. It also has 1536 CUDA cores, 128 texture units, and 32 ROPs. The GeForce GTX 680 has 2 GB of GDDR5 memory. The memory utilizes 256 bit interface, and runs at 1.502 GHz. This allows the card to have 192.26 GB/s memory bandwidth. The GPU has PCI Express 3.0 interface, and needs 2 motherboard slots. The maximum power draw of this model is 195 Watt, on a par with the reference design. The Point of View GeForce GTX 680 is compatible with SLI technology, and will perform considerably better in games when used with similar GPUs (up to 3 in total).
